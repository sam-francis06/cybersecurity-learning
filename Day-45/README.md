Day 45 focused on strengthening practical cybersecurity skills through hands-on Linux security and problem-solving challenges. I completed the **The Hollow Shell** TryHackMe challenge, gaining practical experience in Linux enumeration, system investigation, service discovery, and privilege escalation techniques. Working through the room reinforced a structured methodology for identifying attack surfaces and solving Capture The Flag (CTF) scenarios using systematic enumeration.

I also completed **bytemancy 2** and **ABSOLUTE NANO** on **CyLab Security Academy**, where I analyzed binary and hexadecimal data, interpreted encoded information, and applied logical reasoning to solve practical cybersecurity challenges. These exercises strengthened my analytical mindset and improved my ability to investigate technical problems by extracting meaningful information from limited data.

Together, today's activities enhanced my understanding of Linux security, binary analysis, logical reasoning, and structured problem solving while continuing to expand my practical cybersecurity experience through hands-on learning.
