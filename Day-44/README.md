## About Day 44

Day 44 focused on strengthening practical cybersecurity skills through hands-on cryptography and digital forensics challenges. I completed the **CryptoCabana** TryHackMe challenge, gaining practical experience with classical cryptographic techniques, encryption and decryption methods, encoding and decoding schemes, and ciphertext analysis. Working through the challenge reinforced a systematic approach to solving cryptographic problems commonly encountered in Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions.

I also completed the **c0rrupt** challenge on **CyLab Security Academy**, where I analyzed corrupted data, recovered useful information, and applied logical reasoning to solve a practical cybersecurity problem. This challenge further developed my investigative mindset and strengthened my ability to examine files, identify anomalies, and extract meaningful data during forensic-style analysis.

Together, today's activities enhanced my understanding of cryptography, digital forensics, and structured problem solving while expanding my practical cybersecurity experience through hands-on learning.
