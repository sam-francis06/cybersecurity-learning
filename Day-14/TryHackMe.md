## Walkthrough Rooms

### Cryptography Concepts

**Category:** Cryptography
**Type:** Walkthrough Room

**Overview**

This room covered the fundamental principles used to protect information through encryption, encoding, hashing, and secure communication, providing foundational knowledge for understanding modern cryptographic systems and their applications in cybersecurity.

**Concepts Covered**

- Cryptography fundamentals
- Plaintext and ciphertext
- Encryption and decryption
- Cryptographic keys
- Symmetric encryption
- Asymmetric encryption
- Hashing
- Digital signatures
- Secure communication

**Key Takeaways**

- Cryptography protects the confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of information.
- Encryption transforms plaintext into ciphertext using cryptographic algorithms and keys.
- Symmetric encryption uses the same key for encryption and decryption.
- Asymmetric encryption uses mathematically related public and private keys.
- Hash functions generate fixed-length outputs and are designed to be one-way operations.
- Digital signatures help verify message authenticity and integrity.
- Cryptographic fundamentals are essential for web security, network security, and secure system design.
