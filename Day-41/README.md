## About Day 41

Day 41 emphasized practical cybersecurity skills through a combination of foundational learning, investigative thinking, and hands-on challenge solving. The **Data Encoding** walkthrough strengthened understanding of common data encoding formats, binary and hexadecimal representations, character encoding, and decoding techniques that frequently appear in penetration testing, digital forensics, and CTF competitions. The **Overheard at Breakfast** TryHackMe challenge required systematic enumeration, information gathering, analytical reasoning, and structured problem-solving to identify and exploit available clues. Completing **tunn3l v1s10n** on **CyLab Security Academy** further developed steganography and digital forensics skills by identifying and extracting concealed information from image files. Together, today's activities improved technical investigation skills while reinforcing essential concepts used in offensive security, forensic analysis, and Capture The Flag competitions.
